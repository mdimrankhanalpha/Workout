Pull-Up
Lats. 5-8 reps.
Hang from the bar with palms facing away. Pull your chin over the bar, then lower slowly.
-
Bent-Over Row
Back. 10 reps.
Hinge forward at the hips with a flat back. Pull the weights toward your ribs and lower them.
-
Superman
Lower back. Hold 30 seconds.
Lie face down. Lift your arms and legs off the floor, hold briefly, then relax.
-
Dumbbell Shrug
Traps. 12-15 reps.
Hold the dumbbells at your sides. Lift your shoulders toward your ears, then lower them.
