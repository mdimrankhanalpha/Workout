Hamstring Stretch
Stretching. 30 seconds each leg.
Sit with one leg extended. Reach toward your toes until you feel a gentle stretch.
-
Hip Circles
Warm-up. 10 each direction.
Stand with hands on hips. Move your hips in large slow circles.
-
Child's Pose
Cool-down. 45 seconds.
Kneel and sit back on your heels. Reach your arms forward and rest your forehead down.
