Bodyweight Squat
Quads. 15 reps.
Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Sit your hips back and down, then stand up.
-
Romanian Deadlift
Hamstrings. 10 reps.
Hold the weights in front of your thighs. Push your hips back with a flat back, then return to standing.
-
Side-Lying Leg Raise
Abductors. 15 reps each side.
Lie on your side. Lift your top leg upward, then lower it slowly.
-
Adductor Squeeze
Adductors. 15 reps.
Lie on your back with knees bent. Squeeze a pillow between your knees and release.
