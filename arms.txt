Biceps. 3 sets of 10-12 reps.
Hold the dumbbells at your sides. Curl the weights upward and slowly lower them.

Triceps Dip
Triceps. 8-12 reps.
Place your hands on a bench behind you. Lower your body by bending your elbows, then press back up.
-
Wrist Curl
Forearms. 15 reps.
Rest your forearms on your thighs, palms up. Curl the weights with your wrists.
