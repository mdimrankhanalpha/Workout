Burpee
Full body. 10 reps.
Squat, jump your feet back to a plank, do a push-up, jump forward, then jump up.
-
Kettlebell Swing
Full body. 15 reps.
Hinge at the hips and swing the kettlebell to chest height by driving your hips forward.
-
Thruster
Full body. 10 reps.
Squat with weights at your shoulders. Stand up and press the weights overhead in one motion.
