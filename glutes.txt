name: Glute Bridge
type: Strength
difficulty: Beginner
target: Glutes
equipment: None
reps: 15
instructions: Lie on your back with knees bent. Lift your hips until your body forms a straight line, then lower.

name: Hip Thrust
type: Strength
difficulty: Intermediate
target: Glutes
equipment: Bench, Dumbbell
reps: 10-12
instructions: Rest your upper back on a bench with a weight on your hips. Drive your hips up, then lower.
