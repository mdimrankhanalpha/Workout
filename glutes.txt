Glute Bridge
Glutes. 15 reps.
Lie on your back with knees bent. Lift your hips until your body forms a straight line, then lower.
-
Hip Thrust
Glutes. 10-12 reps.
Rest your upper back on a bench with a weight on your hips. Drive your hips up, then lower.
-
Donkey Kick
Glutes. 12 reps each side.
On hands and knees, kick one heel toward the ceiling, then return.
