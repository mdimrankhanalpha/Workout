Push-Up
Chest. 10-15 reps.
Place your hands under your shoulders with your body straight. Lower your chest to the floor and push back up.
-
Dumbbell Bench Press
Chest. 3 sets of 8-10 reps.
Lie on a bench holding the dumbbells at chest level. Press them up, then lower with control.
-
Incline Push-Up
Upper chest. 10-12 reps.
Place your hands on a raised surface and perform a push-up.
