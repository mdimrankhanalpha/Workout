Oatmeal with Banana
Breakfast. About 350 calories.
Cook 1/2 cup oats in 1 cup milk for 5 minutes. Top with sliced banana and a little honey.

Grilled Chicken and Rice
Lunch. About 550 calories and 45 g protein.
Grill 150 g chicken breast until cooked through. Serve with 1 cup rice and steamed broccoli.

Greek Yogurt with Berries
Snack. About 180 calories and 15 g protein.
Mix 1 cup Greek yogurt with 1/2 cup berries.
