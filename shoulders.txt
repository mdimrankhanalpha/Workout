name: Overhead Press
type: Strength
difficulty: Intermediate
target: Shoulders
equipment: Dumbbells
reps: 8-10
instructions: Hold the dumbbells at shoulder height. Press them overhead and lower with control.

type: Mobility
difficulty: Beginner
target: Neck
equipment: None
