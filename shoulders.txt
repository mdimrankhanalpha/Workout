Overhead Press
Shoulders. 8-10 reps.
Hold the dumbbells at shoulder height. Press them overhead and lower with control.
-
Lateral Raise
Side delts. 12-15 reps.
Raise the dumbbells out to your sides to shoulder height, then lower slowly.
-
Neck Tilt
Neck. 20 seconds each side.
Gently tilt your ear toward your shoulder and hold. Repeat on the other side.
